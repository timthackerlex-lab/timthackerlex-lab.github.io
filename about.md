<p>I’m Timothy Thacker, a writer focused on the intersection of human nature, technology, and the future.</p>
<p>My current work explores the rise of artificial intelligence and the deeper implications it may have on society, power, and control.</p>
<p>At the heart of these stories are questions about control, truth, and the choices people make when the future is uncertain.</p>
<p>My upcoming novel follows a man who begins to uncover what those in power know about artificial intelligence—and what they plan to do with it.</p>
